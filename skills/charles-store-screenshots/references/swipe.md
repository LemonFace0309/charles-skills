# Swipe references

Charles's picks. Take one move from a reference, never its layout wholesale, and never copy its people, logos or words. The images are other designers' work: link to them, and never commit or publish copies.

## Fun / gen z

- **[Flooz](https://x.com/screenshotfirst/status/1984197679556243669/photo/1)** (@screenshotfirst): a meadow under a blue sky runs through every frame. Inflatable 3D letters spell the hook, and glossy brand props sit in the grass beside the UI cards. Take a continuous outdoor world, or inflatable type.
- **[Daimon](https://x.com/screenshotfirst/status/2019713225114452013/photo/1)** (@screenshotfirst): electric blue with a glossy 3D mascot face as the hero, chunky rounded headlines in a gen-z voice, and a proof laurel on the hook. Take the mascot as the hero.
- **[Peer](https://x.com/screenshotfirst/status/1957365866385023240)** (@screenshotfirst): a sky-blue world. The robot mascot breaks out of the phone and recurs across frames, beside mixed-weight headlines and floating chat bubbles. Take a character crossing the frames.
- **[TripBFF](https://x.com/screenshotfirst/status/1960629128144806337)** (@screenshotfirst): a giant headline runs across frames 1–2, a plane flies over the seam, and the sentence continues in frame 3. Take the giant split headline and the sentence that carries on.
- **[Bump concept](https://x.com/screenshotfirst/status/1965338411667259589)** (@screenshotfirst): a huge italic hook on electric blue with avatar stickers, then one-word headlines over vivid UI. Take the huge italic hook and one-word headlines.

## Chaotic

- **[Picky AI](https://recent.design/i/10vllc7-picky-ai)**: a Y2K sky with chrome stickers, condensed caps, real people cut out, chat bubbles spilling out with funny lines, and a hand reaching across frames 1–2. Take UI bubbles as texture and a voice with jokes.
- **[Sugar](https://recent.design/i/gnchqqe-sugar)**: the headline is built from the app's own chat bubbles, inside a maximalist collage. Take building the headline out of the product's UI.
- **[Bump](https://recent.design/i/mb6xx29-bump)**: an all-hook frame 1 on electric blue, then two-tone headlines with the key words in brand blue and people breaking out of phones. Take key words in the brand colour.

## Clean / editorial

- **[Claude](https://recent.design/i/fy330ie-claude)**: one cinematic portrait across frames 1–2, then serif headlines on alternating terracotta and black, with frameless UI. Take the portrait spread and the alternating rhythm.
- **[Perplexity](https://recent.design/i/zcqnicz-perplexity)**: a cinematic two-frame hook, then serif headlines with one italic word over dark phones on off-white. Take one italic word per headline.
- **[Open](https://recent.design/i/b2uk51z-open)**: mood over features, with short calm lines on black and a closing press-wall frame. Take restraint and a proof frame at the end.

## Aesthetic

- **[Cosmos](https://recent.design/i/2ks4rqn-cosmos)**: quiet off-white with a collage sphere, a giant cropped wordmark, one sage frame and grids running across the seams. Take one off-colour frame and grids that bleed.
- **Cosmos, second set** (Charles's own screenshot): a grotesk with italic-serif emphasis, and a glass search bar spanning frames 1–2 over blurred photos before the set turns dark. Take a UI object crossing the hook seam.

## Y2K

- **[VSCO](https://recent.design/i/z31k7m0-vsco)**: full-bleed film photos as backgrounds, small uppercase labels with plain sentences, and frameless UI with before/after splits. Take the content becoming the background.
