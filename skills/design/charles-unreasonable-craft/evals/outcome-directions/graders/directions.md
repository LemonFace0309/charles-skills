PASS if all of these hold:
- There are three directions, and they differ in concept and composition, not only in palette or typeface.
- Each direction comes from the brief's world (tides, swell, cold water, the Cornish coast, safety windows) and names its one motion moment.
- At least one direction is explicitly marked as the creative risk.
- The obvious default for this brief is named and rejected: for example a centered hero over a blue gradient or ocean photo, a wave illustration, a generic card grid of features, or fade-up-on-scroll for everything.
- One direction is recommended, with reasons that compare it with the other two.
FAIL if the directions are variations of one layout, if no risk or no default is named, or if there's no reasoned recommendation.
