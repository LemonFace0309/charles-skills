# Sprout: brief for the App Store screenshot set

**The app:** Sprout is a habit tracker where each habit is a plant on your windowsill. Check a habit off and its plant grows; skip three days and it wilts, unless you use a streak freeze.

**Who it's for:** people in their 20s and 30s who've tried habit apps before and keep quitting them.

**Personality:** warm, a little cheeky, never preachy.

**Features, ranked by what users say they value (reviews and the paywall survey):**
1. The windowsill: every habit is a plant that grows as you keep it
2. Streak freeze: one tap saves a streak on a bad day
3. Lock-screen check-in: tick a habit off without opening the app
4. The Sunday review: a week at a glance, with what grew and what wilted
5. Nudges from friends who share a habit with you
