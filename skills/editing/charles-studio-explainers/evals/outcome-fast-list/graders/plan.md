The known answers for a fast list (about 1.4 s an item):
- No card has a Before tile, a Before half or Before/After labels: the After fills the whole card.
- Each card shows a real moment from an app or game people know, recreated (for example a streak about to reset, a daily prize wheel, an App Store ratings card, a game's dice running out, a calendar icon flipping to Monday, a paywall with a struck anchor price), or a real-world object that puns on the principle. Not a bare UI widget such as a checklist, a leaderboard, plain price rows or a stats card.
- No two cards in a row share a colour family, and no two light stages (white, grey, cream or a pale tint) sit next to each other. A white app screen next to a light card goes on a coloured stage.

PASS if all three hold for all six cards. FAIL if any card has a Before, any card is only a generic UI widget, or any two neighbouring cards share a colour family or are both light.
