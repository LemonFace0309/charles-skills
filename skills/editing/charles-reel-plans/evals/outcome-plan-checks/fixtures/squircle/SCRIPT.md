**Why every app icon is a squircle**

Every app icon on your phone is the same shape.
And it's not actually a rounded square.
Apple switched to a shape called a squircle back in 2013 with iOS 7.
Its curve starts earlier, so the corners blend into the sides.
Your eye reads that smooth curve as one calm shape.
A plain rounded square has a visible kink where the corner meets the side.
