# Earth textures

- `earth_day_4096.jpg`, `earth_night_4096.jpg`, `earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg`: from
  [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) (CC BY 4.0), resized and merged by the three.js
  project (red = bump, green = roughness, blue = clouds). Credit "Earth textures: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0)" in
  a published video's description when they are visible.
- `earth_lights_2048.png`: city lights from the three.js examples, after NASA's Earth at Night (public domain).
